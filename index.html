THE COASTLINE WORTH THE DETOUR

Cabo de Gata.
Algarve.
The Atlantic edge.

The scenery is concentrated where it belongs: La Amatista and the volcanic coves of Cabo de Gata; Ponta da Piedade, Praia da Marinha and Cape St Vincent in the Algarve; Costa Vicentina and Cabo da Roca on the way to Lisbon; Nazaré before Porto.

DAY BY DAY

The route,
without the rush.

Swipe or drag to explore
31 Oct

Freiburg

+

Poland → Freiburg. Pure transit day.

Historical weatherTypical 14°C / 6°C at the October–November boundary. Cool, often cloudy or misty; showers are quite possible.

1 Nov

Cornellà de Llobregat

+

Freiburg → Barcelona airport side. Pick-up option at El Prat; skip Barcelona sightseeing.

Historical weatherTypical 17°C / 8°C. Usually mild with mixed sun and cloud; around 5 wet days in November.

2–3 Nov

Valencia

+

Arrival walk, then one full day for the old town, market and City of Arts and Sciences.

Historical weatherTypical 20°C / 10°C. Often pleasantly sunny; rain is infrequent but can arrive as a short, heavy Mediterranean downpour.

4 Nov

Cabo de Gata → Málaga

+

A long but worthwhile transfer: volcanic Cabo de Gata viewpoints, then continue west to Málaga. Almería is no longer an overnight stop.

Historical weatherTypical 20°C / 11°C around Málaga; Cabo de Gata is usually drier but exposed and windy. Roughly 5 wet days per month in Málaga.

5 Nov

Málaga

+

A complete Málaga day with the car left at the apartment; Nerja can be added on arrival only if energy and daylight allow.

Historical weatherTypical 20°C / 11°C. Mild enough for terraces in sunshine, but November showers can be intense when they occur.

6–7 Nov

Seville

+

Málaga → Mirador del Estrecho and optional Tarifa or Baelo Claudia → Seville. Remain entirely in Spain; one full day in Seville.

Historical weatherTypical 20°C / 9°C. Comfortable sightseeing weather; about 6 wet days per month. The Strait coast can feel much windier.

8–9 Nov

Lagos

+

Praia do Barril en route, followed by a full Algarve cliff day.

Historical weatherTypical 20°C / 11°C. Mild but Atlantic-exposed: sun, fast-moving showers and strong cliff-top wind can all occur in one day.

10–12 Nov

Lisbon

+

Costa Vicentina and Cabo da Roca on arrival, then two full Lisbon days with the car secured at the apartment.

Historical weatherTypical 18°C / 12°C. November is one of Lisbon’s wetter months—around 10 wet days—with occasional windy Atlantic fronts.

13–15 Nov

Porto

+

Nazaré cliffs on the transfer, then two complete days in Porto.

Historical weatherTypical 17°C / 8°C. High rain risk: roughly 13 wet days and about 160 mm in November. Expect damp, breezy spells between brighter breaks.

16 Nov

Bilbao

+

Porto → Bilbao together. The hotel is close to the motorway and the old town is reachable by bus; this is the final shared night before the Bilbao flight.

Historical weatherTypical 16°C / 8°C. Atlantic and frequently wet—about 12 rainy days and 140 mm during November.

17 Nov

Bourges

+

Bilbao airport drop-off, then continue solo to Bourges. Roughly eight hours of driving before any airport detour, with breakfast ready before departure the next morning.

Historical weatherTypical 11°C / 5°C. Grey, humid and often showery; around 12 wet days. Morning fog and an occasional light frost are possible.

18 Nov

Nuremberg

+

Bourges → Nuremberg. Private surface-style hotel parking avoids another awkward underground ramp.

Historical weatherTypical 8°C / 2°C. Cold, often overcast and damp; around 7 wet days, with frost and the first wet snow possible.

19 Nov

Kraków

+

Nuremberg → Kraków. Final drive home without pushing the trip into 20 November.

Historical weatherTypical 8°C / 1°C. Late-autumn cold with a modest rain risk; frost, fog and occasional rain–snow mix become possible by mid-month.

These are planning normals, not a 2026 forecast. Temperatures are typical November daytime highs / overnight lows. Sources: AEMET-based Spain normals, IPMA-based Portugal normals, Météo-France and published long-term climate averages for Freiburg, Nuremberg and Kraków.
